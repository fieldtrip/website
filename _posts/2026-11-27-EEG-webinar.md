In a recent [BP Academy Webinar](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLgBQatx-tYbBoO69VrVF8GSuN2l0odDMo), Robert Oostenveld and Cyril Pernet discuss with Tracy Warbrick how to make EEG research more transparent, reliable, and reproducible, addressing challenges in experimental design, data acquisition, analysis, and interpretation. In the webinar we explore practical approaches to improving standardisation, statistical reliability, transparent reporting, and data sharing, including the role of BIDS and open-science tools.
