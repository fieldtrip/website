### 11 November, 2026

Alaaf, narri, etc.! We have released FieldTrip version [20261111](http://github.com/fieldtrip/fieldtrip/releases/tag/20261111). Chockfull of small improvements that haven't made it into the official release announcements. It's been almost a year since the last time stamped version has been mentioned here, but a lot of small things have changed/improved to keep the codebase at the forefront of your electrophysiological data analysis endeavours. 

Some highlights are:
- continued improvements for the support of various aspects of OPM-MEG data, including denoising strategies and custom helmet design
- improvements on spectral estimation, including adaptive multitaper weighting with Slepian tapers, and more algorithmic flexibility for the IRASA technique
- improved gateway functionality to interface with Matthias Treder's [MVPA-light toolbox](https://github.com/treder/MVPA-Light), to allow for the algorithmic improvements supported by MVPA-light, including more flexible cross-decoding, searchlight analysis, and custom train/test models
- improved and faster implementation of TFCE for type-1 statistical error control

Some other things to mention:
- we have also welcomed PRs from colleagues that were helped by their interns [Claude](https://github.com/fieldtrip/fieldtrip/pull/2610) and others. We do appreciate this trend, but would like to mention that we greatly value the human aspect of the collaboration, and hope that you as a contributor keep that in mind
- also, although this is not strictly a part of the codebase as such, we have made various improvements to the website, one of which is a landing page for external contributions (a.k.a. [extensions](https://www.fieldtriptoolbox.org/extensions/) to the FieldTrip ecosystem. This hopefully improves the visibility of your work 

See [GitHub](https://github.com/fieldtrip/fieldtrip/compare/20251201...20261111) for the detailed list of updates.
