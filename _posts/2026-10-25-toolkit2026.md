### 25 October 2026

From 12-16 October 2026 we hosted our annual MEG toolkit course in Nijmegen. This year was the first time we had a strong focus on OPMs. We covered general MEG preprocessing, source reconstruction and statistics and gave special attention to new OPM-specific methods for [denoising](/tutorial/preproc/denoising_opm), [coregistration](/tutorial/source/coregistration_opm), and custom [helmet design](/tutorial/sensor/opm_helmet_design) for OPMs.

We recorded all lectures on video, and together with the slides you can find those on the [workshop page](/workshop/toolkit2026).
