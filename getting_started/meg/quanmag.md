QuanMag is a Chinese company based in Beijing that develops and manufactures optically pumped magnetometer (OPM)-based MEG systems. Their helium-free quantum MEG technology uses arrays of highly sensitive atomic magnetometers to measure magnetic fields generated by neuronal activity, without requiring cryogenic cooling. The systems include both stationary and wearable configurations, with adaptable sensor arrays designed for different head sizes, including infants and children. Their technology is aimed at both fundamental neuroscience research and clinical applications, offering opportunities for studying brain function, localizing epileptic activity, and investigating brain development and brain–computer interfaces.

A distinctive feature of their system is a compact, person-sized magnetically shielded enclosure, resembling a small MRI scanner, which provides a low-noise environment for MEG recordings without requiring a conventional walk-in magnetically shielded room. The system combines this compact shielding solution with an OPM sensor array, offering a relatively small-footprint platform for fundamental neuroscience research and clinical applications, as demonstrated on the BIOMAG 2026 conference.
